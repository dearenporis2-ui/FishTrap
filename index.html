A family fish trap, brought back to life

In the 1980s, the Mills brothers' uncle ran the original Fish Trap on Praslin. Today the brothers have reopened it in the middle of La Passe.

The bamboo fish trap, known locally as the kazye, is still the heart of the room. You'll see it woven into the decor, set against marine blues and greens that look like the water outside.

It's a place for locals and visitors alike, on La Digue's busiest corner.

Getting here

We're in the centre of La Passe, the island's main harbour and the easiest place on La Digue to find.

By ferry

Boats arrive at the La Passe jetty, and we're a short walk from it, near the main harbour. Step off, look for the busiest corner in town, and you're there.

On foot

La Passe is small and easy to walk. You don't need a car to reach us from the jetty or from most of the town.

By bike

Bikes are the way to get around La Digue. Cycling in from the beaches or the rest of the island, you'll find us right in the middle of La Passe.
